1. **The World, with Globals.** The kernel accepts the kit v0.11 dict: `locals` and `globals` as named dimensions, a state the pair `(l, g)`, `prior_global` and `prior_local` = P(local | Global) as the declared Prior's two factors. A dict with no Globals is a v0.1 World and must play exactly as before: every earlier suite of the kit stays green (C21).
2. **The prior from Counts (§4).** P(Global | Counts) ∝ P(Global) · Π L(record | Global)^count, where L sums the local out under P(local | Global) over every draw of the record and its after-report. The **record** is the unit, never the draw, because the draws of one episode share its local. Exact rationals throughout; no float, no log, no approximation. It is v0's Bayes' theorem applied to records, not a second learning mechanism: nothing is fitted, nothing is forgotten, nothing depends on the order the records arrived (C22, C23).
3. **The plate.** A new module (`src/wald/plate.py`, say) holding one `Plate`: its Counts, its falsifying record, nothing else — no log, no cache with a meaning (S13). `Plate.run(door)` is one episode: the prior from the Counts, v0's loop at the floor unchanged, the terminal fired, then — if the World declares an After-act — its report taken **whenever declared** (J27), asked of the door by the After-act's name after the fire. `decide` never reads the After-act's kernel and never credits an act with what the grade would teach (S12, J21). The record enters the Counts. A report of probability zero, during the episode or after it, ends the plate WORLD_FALSIFIED: the Counts stand, the falsifying record is kept beside them (J26), and a further `run` is refused. `wald.plate` is the one new public name.
4. **Refusals, by name**, in `world.declare` for a v0.2 dict: `GLOBAL` (a utility or ending utility reads a Global, S11); `AFTER` (a second After-act, a kernel that omits an end or reads more than the state and the end, S12); `PLATE` (shipped Counts whose sha-256 is not the declared one — computed exactly as `counts_check.counts_sha` does, which SURFACE v0.2 will adopt as the canonical encoding — or holding a record no episode of this World can write under v0's episode mechanics, or a multiset, the falsifier included, impossible under every Global value, S13); `UNSCORED` (a shipped Score that is not the leave-one-out predictive probability, S14). **S15 refuses nothing.**
5. **S15's disclosure.** Inseparable Global values over realisable designs (sequences of acts within N, `once` at most once, ending outcomes ending them, then an end, then the After-act), grouped into classes. Say first, when there is one class, that nothing declared as a Global can be learned. Then list every class whose members disagree about what an act can feel of the state — the differences between terminal utilities, and best(state) when the think act is declared — jointly with a design's draws, the after-report left out. `Plate.disclosure()` gives it as inert text; the adapter gives it structured for the kit. This computation is exponential in N; the kit's Worlds are small. Say in KERNEL.md how it scales and do not approximate it (CLAUDE.md: a performance problem is a stop, not an approximation).
6. **E7's lines and the Score.** For every draw in the Counts, grouped by the history within its episode that led to it: the total variation between the empirical distribution of its outcome and its posterior predictive — the Global weighted by P(Global | all Counts), the history conditioning the local only. The Score: the product, over each copy of each record, of its likelihood under the prior conditioned on all the others. Both exact.
7. **The adapter** gains `prior`, `persist`, `declare`, `disclose`, `e7`, `score` and `world`, each a line of conversion around one kernel call (INTERFACE.md). It chooses nothing.
8. **`wald.__all__` lists all eleven public names** — the six of kit v0.1, the four of brief 006 (your Q5, now answered), and `plate`. ST1 allows exactly these.
