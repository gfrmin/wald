**The owner's and author's numbers for this brief** (you choose none):

  - twins124: `a = 99613375741023143/629231233233738`, `b = 22716401291570095/314615616616869`, `score = 149/500`.

1. **Two packs**, `packs/wordle200/adaptive.py` and `packs/twins/adaptive.py`, from `tools/make_wordle_pack.py` with its new `--think=a,b,rate,score` (already in this branch's tool; comma-separated because `b` is negative). Each is the depth-1 Wordle pack on its lexicon plus, at the end, `depth_plus(2, source="elicited")`, `think(fraction=1/2, source="elicited")`, two `fitted` params `cost_a`, `cost_b`, `cost([...])` of exactly |Ω| cells written as arithmetic over them (SURFACE v0.1 K2, K12), `rate(1/10000000, source="elicited")`, `score(…, of="cost", source="data")`. Commit both packs; regenerate, do not edit.
2. **Play them.** `tools/play_wordle.py` gains the adaptive packs: for every answer, the acts, the S7 buckets, `thought` and `operations` from `Result`. The kit's T2 asks that every act, every bucket and every thought charged equal the meta oracle's on sampled answers; a kernel that thinks where the oracle is struck, or plays the depth-1 act after paying, fails there.
3. **`packs/wordle200/SCOREBOARD.md` and `packs/twins/SCOREBOARD.md`**, each with, at the declared r: attempts distribution, mean, worst, wrong claims, thought paid, predicted against realised operations per think act (E6; the counter's realised number is what it is — memo hits make later thoughts cheap, say so), the four S7 counts, the census (including the fitted count), wall-clock. Then the **five curves** along the r grid — fixed d=1, fixed d=2 paying c, best fixed depth in hindsight, adaptive, omniscient — as the meta oracle computes them exactly; the kit prints the same table, and yours must match it. Then one line per lexicon: the regret of the adaptive agent against the omniscient meta-policy at the declared r, and against the best fixed depth.
4. **Report what it says.** The author's oracle says: on words200 every curve but the adaptive one is −2.6450 at every r and the adaptive agent overpays below r ≈ 3/10⁷; on twins124 fixed d=1 (−3.4194) beats free depth 2 (−3.4516) and the omniscient gains 0.016 by thinking at one child of the root. Do not tune anything to change this. If your kernel's episodes and the oracle disagree on an act, a bucket or a cost, that is a `QUESTIONS.md` entry with the answer and both traces: one of us is wrong, and it may be the oracle.
5. **Timing.** The whole cage command inside CI's 30 minutes. A root thought on 200 words is one cold depth-2 evaluation (~37 s here); the kit samples 5 answers per lexicon.

Nothing in `src/wald` changes for this brief unless the kit says so. No Wordle in the kernel.
